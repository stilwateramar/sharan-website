Team & Doctors

SHARAN is a team of doctors, nutritionists, health coaches, chefs and facilitators who share one commitment: to help you reach your highest health potential.

Founder

Dr. Nandita Shah

Dr. Nandita Shah is a homeopath, author and health educator who has spent more than three decades teaching that most chronic disease is rooted in lifestyle and is reversible. She founded SHARAN in 2005.

In 2016 she received the Nari Shakti Puraskar, the highest civilian honour for women in India, awarded by the President of India.
