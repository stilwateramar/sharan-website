About SHARAN

A two-decade movement to bring holistic health, compassion and ecological sanity into everyday Indian life.

Who We Are

Health, compassion, and the planet — one practice.

SHARAN — Sanctuary for Health and Reconnection to Animals and Nature — is a non-profit founded in 2005 by Dr. Nandita Shah. We exist because most modern disease is rooted in modern lifestyle, and because the same choices that heal our bodies also tend to be kinder to animals and gentler on the earth.

Our team includes doctors, nutritionists, health coaches, chefs and educators. Together we run programs, workshops, retreats, talks and one-on-one consultations across India and online — with a growing community of alumni who carry the practice forward.

"Most chronic disease is not a life sentence. It is a daily set of choices we have been taught to make — and can be taught to unmake."

— SHARAN guiding principle

What we do

  • Run structured programs to prevent and reverse Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, obesity and other lifestyle conditions.
  • Teach plant-based, whole-food cooking through hands-on classes and online courses.
  • Host workshops, retreats and public talks on holistic health and compassionate living.
  • Provide doctor and nutritionist consultations for individuals and families.
  • Publish books and free educational material to make this knowledge accessible.

Our values

Ahimsa. Non-violence is the thread that runs through everything we do — towards your body, towards animals, and towards the natural world.

Evidence. We teach what is supported by clinical experience and peer-reviewed nutrition science, not fads.

Accessibility. We work to make this knowledge available across language, income and geography.

Joy. Healthy living should taste good and feel good. If it doesn't, we are doing it wrong.
