Donate

SHARAN runs as a non-profit. Your support keeps public talks free, opens scholarship places on programs, and makes books and guides available at cost.

Where your gift goes

  • Free and low-cost public talks across India.
  • Scholarship places for participants who need financial support.
  • Distribution of books and printed guides at cost.
  • Educational outreach in schools, colleges and community groups.
  • Translation of material into Indian languages.

Ways to give

One-time gift — UPI, bank transfer or card.

Monthly giving — a small recurring amount goes a long way; it's the steadiest support we can receive.

Sponsor a participant — fund a single scholarship place on a program.

In-kind — venues, printing, design, translation and volunteer hours.
