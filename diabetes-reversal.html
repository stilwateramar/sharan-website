Reversal Program

Diabetes Reversal

For people with Type 2 Diabetes, pre-diabetes or insulin resistance — a structured program to bring blood sugar down and reduce dependency on medication.

What this program is

This is a guided journey, not a diet. Over several weeks, you learn how Type 2 Diabetes develops in the body, what makes it worsen, and which everyday choices reverse it. You eat real food, cooked at home, with growing skill and confidence. A doctor and nutritionist are with you the whole way.

Who it is for

  • Adults living with Type 2 Diabetes — newly diagnosed or long-standing.
  • Pre-diabetics with rising HbA1c, fasting sugar or insulin resistance.
  • Family members of people with diabetes who want to prevent it.
  • Anyone whose doctor has flagged metabolic health concerns.

What you can expect

  • Daily lessons explaining the why behind each food choice.
  • Plant-based, whole-food meal plans built for Indian kitchens.
  • Group calls with a doctor and nutritionist, plus written guidance.
  • Tracking of fasting glucose, post-meal glucose and HbA1c trends.
  • An alumni community to keep your habits alive after the program ends.

"I came in on three medications. By the end of the program, on advice from my own doctor, I was off two of them — and my fasting sugar was the lowest it had been in a decade."

— Diabetes Reversal alum (illustrative)

How to start

The next intake date and fees are listed on the registration page. If you want to discuss your situation before signing up, write to us — a team member will respond within two working days.
