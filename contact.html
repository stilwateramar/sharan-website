Contact us

Whether you want to enrol in a program, host a talk, ask a medical question or partner with us — we'd love to hear from you.

How can we help?

Use the form to send a message. For specific topics, mention the keyword in your note so the right team member picks it up:

  • Programs — Diabetes, Hypertension, Weight Release, Healthy Cooking
  • Workshops & talks — host one for your community or company
  • Retreats — ask about upcoming dates and locations
  • Books — orders, requests, free guides
  • Volunteer — offer your time or skills
  • Partner — clinical, corporate, NGO, media

Reach us also by email, phone and the social handles in the footer. Replace these placeholders with your live contact details.
