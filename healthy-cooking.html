Healthy Cooking

Learn to make plant-based food that you and your family actually look forward to — at breakfast, at lunch, at dinner, and at every snack in between.

In-person classes

Small-group, hands-on classes in cities across India. Cook together, taste together, and walk out with recipes you can repeat the same week.

Upcoming dates announced on the contact page.

Online cooking courses

Video-led courses you can follow at your own pace, designed for the home cook with a normal Indian kitchen and pantry — no fancy equipment.

Available year-round.

What you will be able to make

  • Whole-grain breakfasts that don't crash your energy by eleven.
  • Dals, sabzis and one-pot meals that feel familiar and feel light.
  • Plant-based "dairy" — milks, curds and cheeses — from nuts and seeds.
  • Sweets and snacks that genuinely qualify as healthy.
  • Travel and lunchbox food that holds up away from a kitchen.

Who teaches

Classes are led by SHARAN-trained chefs and educators, with input from our medical and nutrition team. The focus is on food that is delicious first and healthy second — because if it isn't tasty, none of this lasts.
