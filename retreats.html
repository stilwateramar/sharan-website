Retreats

Step out of your routine for a few days and into one built around food that heals, mornings that feel calm, and evenings without screens.

What a retreat looks like

Days begin gently — a walk, breath work, a quiet meal. Mornings are for learning: short, clear sessions on nutrition, sleep, stress, ageing and reversal. Afternoons are for cooking, kitchen practice and personal consultations. Evenings are for community, music, and time outside.

Retreats are typically held over four to seven days at locations chosen for clean air, simple food, and proximity to nature.

What you'll come home with

More than a holiday — fewer pills, lighter mornings.

🥗

A reset palate

You'll be surprised how quickly your taste returns to whole, simple food.

🧘

A calmer baseline

Sleep deepens, breathing slows, and the noisy stress baseline quiets down.

🤝

A small community

You leave with a handful of people who are walking the same path.
