Our Approach

Evidence-based lifestyle medicine, taught with care.

Our methods are grounded in peer-reviewed nutrition science and decades of clinical practice. Doctors, nutritionists and health coaches walk alongside you — through education, food, movement, sleep and stress — until new habits feel natural.

🌱

Whole-food, plant-based

Meals built around vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes, nuts and seeds — the foundation of every program we run.

🩺

Medical guidance

Programs are designed and supervised by qualified doctors and nutritionists, with personalised consultations available.

🤝

Compassion at the centre

A way of living that is gentle on your body, on animals, and on the planet. Ahimsa as a daily practice, not a slogan.

Our Founder

Dr. Nandita Shah

SHARAN was founded in 2005 by Dr. Nandita Shah, a homeopath, author and health educator. In 2016 she received the Nari Shakti Puraskar — the highest civilian honour for women in India — awarded by the President of India for her work in public health and animal welfare.

Her message is simple and consistent: most chronic disease is rooted in how we eat and live, and almost all of it is reversible when we return to natural, compassionate choices.
