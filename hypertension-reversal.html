Reversal Program

Hypertension Reversal

A structured way to bring blood pressure into a healthy range — without crash diets, without fear, and without quietly tolerating creeping numbers.

Why a program approach works

Blood pressure responds quickly to a handful of well-chosen daily practices: the right food, the right amount of salt, deeper sleep, slower breath, regular gentle movement and less chronic stress. A program gives you the structure to put all of these in place at once — which is why most people see meaningful change inside a few weeks.

Who it is for

  • Adults with diagnosed hypertension on one or more medications.
  • People with borderline or rising blood pressure readings.
  • Anyone with a strong family history of hypertension or stroke.
  • People with related risks — high cholesterol, central weight, sleep apnea.

What you'll learn

  • The role of whole plants, sodium, potassium and processed foods.
  • How to read labels and rebuild your kitchen for low-sodium cooking.
  • Daily practices for sleep, breathing and stress that move the dial.
  • How to track your own blood pressure honestly and confidently at home.

The arc of the program

①

Foundations

Understanding your numbers and the levers that move them.

②

Practice

Putting food, sleep, breath and movement in place — one week at a time.

③

Hold

Locking in the changes so this is how you live, not what you tried.
