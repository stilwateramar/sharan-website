Programs

Structured journeys — typically several weeks long — designed to help you make changes that hold long after the program ends.

Reversal

Diabetes Reversal

For people living with Type 2 Diabetes, pre-diabetes or insulin resistance. Learn how to reduce blood sugar and dependency on medication using whole-food, plant-based eating and daily lifestyle practice.

Program details → Reversal

Hypertension Reversal

Bring blood pressure into a healthy range — without crash diets — through nutrition, salt awareness, sleep, breath, gentle movement and stress practices.

Program details → Lifestyle

Weight Release

A non-restrictive approach to releasing weight you no longer need. Built on satisfying, plant-based meals, not deprivation or counting calories.

Healthy Cooking

Hands-on cooking classes — in person and online — that teach you how to make plant-based food you actually look forward to eating, again and again.

How a SHARAN program runs

Education, kitchen, community, follow-through.

①

Learn

Daily lessons explaining the why and how of plant-based, whole-food living.

②

Cook

Practical kitchen guidance and recipes you can actually make in an Indian kitchen.

③

Connect

Group support so you don't have to do it alone — your cohort moves together.

④

Hold

Follow-up sessions and alumni access to keep your new habits alive.
