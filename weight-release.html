Weight Release

A non-restrictive approach to releasing weight you no longer need — eating real food, in pleasing amounts, until your body finds its natural set point.

Not another diet

Most diets ask you to fight your appetite. Weight Release does the opposite: it helps you choose foods that satisfy you faster, hold you longer and keep your blood sugar steady. The body responds — usually quietly, week after week — until you stop thinking about weight altogether.

Who it is for

  • Adults carrying weight that affects energy, joints, sleep or self-image.
  • People who have tried many diets and want to step off the cycle.
  • People with metabolic concerns (PCOS, fatty liver, pre-diabetes) where weight is a contributing factor.

What's inside

  • A whole-food, plant-based eating framework that does not count calories.
  • Hands-on meal building — breakfasts, lunches, dinners, snacks.
  • Movement guidance you can actually keep up with.
  • Group support and one-on-one coaching as needed.

"For the first time in twenty years, I am not on a diet — and the weight is going."

— Weight Release alum (illustrative)
